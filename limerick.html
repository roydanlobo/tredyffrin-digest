How this would work as a real product

Limerick runs on the same township website platform as Upper Merion (CivicPlus’s AgendaCenter), so the download and parsing logic already built for Upper Merion should carry over directly — what’s left is running real meetings through the pipeline. Limerick has been in the local news recently over a proposed data center and its proximity to the Limerick Generating Station, which is exactly the kind of decision this project exists to make easier to follow — residents get the plain-language version of what the Board actually discussed and voted on, sourced straight from the official minutes.
