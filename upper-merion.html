How this would work as a real product

Upper Merion runs on a different township website platform than Tredyffrin (CivicPlus’s AgendaCenter rather than Tredyffrin’s own site), so it needed its own way of finding and downloading minutes — that groundwork is done. What's left is the same as it was for Tredyffrin at this stage: process a handful of real meetings to prove it out, then keep it current as new meetings post.

Built as a prototype exploring whether Chester County and Montgomery County residents would benefit from plain-language digests of local government meetings.
