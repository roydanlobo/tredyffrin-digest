Zoning & DevelopmentFinanceParks & Recreation
  • Industrial development approved. The Board voted 4-1 to approve the Lot 7 Industrial Parkway project, with Supervisor Irwin opposed.
  • Indoor sports complex opposed. All supervisors expressed opposition to a proposed 85,500 square foot indoor sports complex at 18 Steinmetz Road, citing that the area is not zoned for this use and unauthorized tree clearing occurred on site.
  • Main Street District redesign discussed. Supervisors agreed to divide the existing Main Street District into three separate zoning areas, with support for mixed-use buildings (retail/restaurant on first floor, residential above) and opposition to more drug stores, drive-throughs, gas stations, and large apartment buildings on Ridge Pike.
  • Tax increase may be needed for 2027. During budget discussions, supervisors acknowledged that revenue is not keeping up with expenses due to inflation and need for additional services, with several board members saying they would support a tax increase for capital projects, fire and EMS, and ongoing inflationary costs.
  • Residents urged fiscal restraint. Residents Martha Bell and Rick Fidler asked the Board to keep taxes low and defer certain wants, while Tim Norwood urged the budget to prioritize Police and Fire.
  • Tricentennial under budget. The Township's Tricentennial event will cost significantly less than the budgeted $75,000 due to successful fundraising efforts.
  • Community Park improvements underway. The rubberized playground surface is almost complete, a new fence will be installed, and a grand reopening is scheduled for Community Day on August 15th.
